# SkyForge Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Team SkyForge fosters a respectful, collaborative, and inclusive environment for students, researchers, embedded engineers, and open-source contributors of all skill levels. We are committed to maintaining a learning culture centered on curiosity, academic rigor, and mutual respect.

## Expected Standards

- **Constructive Collaboration:** Critique designs and code respectfully, providing technical rationales and actionable recommendations.
- **Academic & Engineering Integrity:** Acknowledge external research, citations, and open-source libraries accurately. Never publish misleading benchmark data or claim unverified capabilities.
- **Safety Prioritization:** Encourage safe laboratory practices, physical battery handling precautions, and clear disclaimers regarding drone operations.
- **Welcoming Inclusivity:** Treat all contributors with empathy, irrespective of background, experience level, or affiliation.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, derogatory commentary, discriminatory language, or personal insults.
- Publishing private communications, personal contact details, or non-consensual test imagery.
- Encouraging unsafe or irresponsible drone flight operations in unauthorized or public spaces.

## Reporting & Inquiries

Concerns or incidents may be directed to Team SkyForge leadership (GLA University, Department of Computer Science / AIML, Team ID: 87) via the repository issue tracker or academic mentors.
